FAQ

Common questions

Is AIM Research Hub a medical provider?

No. AIM Research Hub is an educational resource and does not provide diagnosis, treatment, prescribing, dosing, or individualized medical advice.

Does a COA guarantee a product is safe?

No. A COA reports particular analytical results for a tested sample. It should not be interpreted as a guarantee of safety, suitability, sterility, or clinical effectiveness.
