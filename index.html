Important distinction

A research supplier's product page, a certificate of analysis, and a peer-reviewed paper answer different questions. A paper about a molecule does not validate a particular commercial vial, and a COA does not establish clinical safety or effectiveness.

EVIDENCE GUIDE

Read studies with context.

Human clinical

Randomized trials and well-designed clinical studies can answer questions that cell and animal models cannot.

Preclinical

Animal and cell studies can identify mechanisms and hypotheses, but should not be presented as proven human outcomes.

Search, don't cherry-pick

Each compound links to a PubMed literature search so readers can see a broader body of research rather than one hand-selected paper.

ANALYTICAL TESTING

Literature ≠ product verification.

Peer-reviewed research concerns the molecule or intervention studied by those investigators. Commercial product identity, purity, quantity, sterility, and contaminants are separate analytical questions. Review lot-specific documentation independently.
