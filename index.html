EVIDENCE GUIDE

Read studies with context.

Human clinical research

Randomized trials and well-designed clinical studies can answer questions that cell and animal studies cannot.

Preclinical research

Animal and laboratory findings can help explain mechanisms, but do not automatically translate to people.

Commercial verification

Published research concerns the intervention studied by those investigators. Commercial product identity requires separate verification.

ANALYTICAL TESTING

Literature ≠ product verification.

Identity

Consider whether the analytical method is appropriate for confirming sample identity.

Purity vs. quantity

A purity percentage and the amount of material present are different measurements.

Traceability

Look for laboratory identity, testing date, sample identifiers, methods, and limitations.
