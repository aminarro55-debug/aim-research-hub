Important distinction

EVIDENCE GUIDE

Read studies with context.

Human clinical research

Randomized trials and well-designed clinical studies can answer questions that cell and animal studies cannot.

Preclinical research

Laboratory and animal research can help researchers understand biological mechanisms, but findings do not automatically translate to humans.

Published literature

Study design, population, endpoints, conflicts of interest, and replication all matter when interpreting scientific findings.

ANALYTICAL TESTING

Literature ≠ product verification.

Identity

Analytical testing may help determine whether a sample contains the compound it claims to contain.

Purity

Purity measurements describe the composition of the analyzed sample and should not be confused with quantity or clinical safety.

COA context

Review the laboratory, test method, sample information, testing date, and other documentation before interpreting a COA.
