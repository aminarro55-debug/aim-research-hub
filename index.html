TESTING & COAs

How to evaluate analytical documentation

A polished report is not enough. Evaluate what was tested, how it was tested, and whether the documentation is traceable to the sample being discussed.

01

Sample identity

Look for an unambiguous sample or batch identifier and confirm it matches the material being represented.

02

Test method

Identify the analytical method and understand what that method actually measures.

03

Purity vs. quantity

A purity percentage and the total amount of material are different measurements.

04

Laboratory & date

Check laboratory information, report dates, signatures or verification mechanisms, where applicable.

A COA has limits.

A certificate of analysis reports particular test results for a sample. It does not automatically establish sterility, safety, regulatory approval, clinical effectiveness, or suitability for human use.

Does high purity prove identity or safety?

No. Purity, identity, quantity, sterility, and safety are distinct questions. A single headline percentage cannot answer all of them.
