Tirzepatide

Dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist. This page organizes educational literature context and clearly separates published research from commercial-material information.

Evidence overview

Evidence quality depends on study design, population, comparator, duration, endpoints, and whether findings come from human clinical research or preclinical models. Results from one setting should not automatically be generalized to another.

Doses & routes reported in studies

Study spotlight

SURMOUNT-1 long-term analysis — NEJM (2025)

Population/design: Participants with obesity and prediabetes; treatment continued for 176 weeks in the long-term analysis.

Investigational exposure: Investigators studied once-weekly 5 mg, 10 mg, or 15 mg assignments versus placebo.

What it examined: The analysis evaluated sustained weight change, progression to type 2 diabetes, and long-term safety.
