Endocrine research

Tesamorelin

GHRH analogue. This page organizes educational literature context and clearly separates published research from commercial-material information.

Evidence overview

Evidence quality depends on study design, population, comparator, duration, endpoints, and whether findings come from human clinical research or preclinical models. Results from one setting should not automatically be generalized to another.

Doses & routes reported in studies

Study spotlight

Randomized clinical trial — JAMA (2014)

Population/design: 50 antiretroviral-treated adults with HIV and abdominal fat accumulation; 6 months.

Investigational exposure: Investigators studied 2 mg subcutaneously once daily versus placebo.

What it examined: Visceral and liver fat were reduced; the study also monitored glucose-related outcomes.

Study spotlight

Randomized placebo-controlled trial + extension (2010)

Population/design: 404 people with HIV and excess abdominal fat; 12-month study.

Investigational exposure: Investigators studied 2 mg subcutaneously daily.

What it examined: Visceral adipose tissue fell during treatment; benefits were lost after switching to placebo.

Study spotlight

NEJM randomized trial (2007)

Population/design: 412 people with HIV and treatment-associated abdominal fat accumulation; 26 weeks.

Investigational exposure: Investigators studied 2 mg by daily subcutaneous injection versus placebo.

What it examined: Visceral fat and several lipid measures improved; glycemic measures were also monitored.
